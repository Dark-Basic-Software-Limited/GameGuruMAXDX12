Build: game with 3.82 (honour explicitly-stated visuals keys), engine 3c955028 (3.80 reflection).

⚠ C2 IS AMENDED FOR THIS CHANGE. Reasoning, before the numbers exist:

  3.82 stops the old-file performance downgrade from overwriting settings a level's own
  visuals.ini states explicitly. MOST SHIPPED DEMOS HAVE PRE-2024 VISUALS FILES - Switch Escape's
  has 124 keys against the modern 190 - so any demo whose old file explicitly states
  shaderlevels.terrain/entities/vegetation/lighting now KEEPS its stated level instead of being
  stamped LOW. Shaderlevels drive vegetation and object detail, so POLYS can legitimately move.

  PREDICTION: POLYS moves ONLY on demos whose embedded visuals.ini contains a shaderlevels key.
  A move on a demo whose file states no shaderlevels key is NOT explained by this change and is a
  real finding to investigate, not to rationalise.

  The three known non-deterministic demos remain Foggy Forest, The Mystery of Z Island and
  Aztec Game Kit Teaser (notes 3.74).

C3 IS NOT AMENDED. The 4 GB min-spec limit is not negotiable. A demo that now loads at a higher
quality level could plausibly approach it, and if any demo exceeds 4096 MB on a FRESH launch that
is a genuine regression from this change and must be fixed or the change reconsidered.

C1 19/19 and C4 19/19 expected unchanged.
